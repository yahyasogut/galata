Our story

Named after the old quarter by the Golden Horn

Galata is where Istanbul meets the water.

Today Galata is two places with one kitchen spirit.

  • Long breakfasts

    Sucuk and eggs, spinach & feta börek, honey and jams.

  • From the sea

    Calamari, garlic prawns and balık ekmek.

  • Canal views

    Sit by the water and watch the narrowboats pass.
